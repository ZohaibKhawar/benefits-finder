# Ontario benefits and programs

Researched 2026-10-18. Benefit year = July 2026 to June 2027 (based on the **2025** tax return) for
anything paid by the CRA. "Official" = read on canada.ca or ontario.ca. "Secondary" = news, college or
tax-prep sites; check before the app shows the number. "Confirm" = from older knowledge, not yet
re-checked this session.

---

## A. Paid by the CRA after you file taxes

### 1. Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB)

One payment that bundles three credits. You need to qualify for at least one.

- **Payment schedule (official):** annual amount split into 12, paid on the 10th of each month from
  July 2026. If the yearly total is **$500 or less**, it comes as one lump sum in July. You can choose
  to get it all in June 2027 instead (only if over $500).
- **How to apply:** fill in form **ON-BEN (5006-TG)** with your tax return (the box most first-time
  filers and students skip). Tax software asks for rent paid, address, months in residence.

#### 1a. Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit (OEPTC)

- **Who (official):** lived in Ontario on Dec 31, 2025 and, for 2025, at least one of:
  - paid rent or property tax for your main home (or someone paid it for you),
  - paid for a public or non-profit long-term care home,
  - paid home energy costs on a reserve,
  - **lived in a designated university, college or private school residence in Ontario.**
- Age: must be 18+ on Dec 31, or have had a spouse/partner, or be a parent living with a child (confirm;
  the page I read didn't state it).
- **Max for 2026-27 (secondary, 2% indexation):** **$1,307** for ages 18 to 64, **$1,488** for 65+.
  (One site says $1,309 / $1,490. Confirm on CRA.)
- **Students in residence:** only **$25** of occupancy cost counts for the months in a designated
  residence, so a student who lived only in residence gets very little. Off-campus renters are the ones
  who benefit: **20% of rent paid is treated as property tax** for the calculation (confirm).
- **Roommates:** each roommate claims the rent they personally paid.
- Phase-out by income: see the ON-BEN calculation sheets (to add in the data phase).

#### 1b. Northern Ontario Energy Credit (NOEC)

- Lived in **Northern Ontario** on Dec 31, 2025 and paid rent/property tax (official).
- Max (secondary): **$189 single, $290 family** for 2026-27.
- Northern Ontario = districts of Algoma, Cochrane, Kenora, Manitoulin, Nipissing, Parry Sound,
  Rainy River, Sudbury, Thunder Bay, Timiskaming (confirm list). The app can ask "Do you live in
  Northern Ontario?" with a list of cities (Sudbury, Thunder Bay, Sault Ste. Marie, North Bay, Timmins...).

#### 1c. Ontario Sales Tax Credit (OSTC)

- **Official, 2026-27:** up to **$378 per adult and per child**.
- Reduced by **4%** of adjusted net income over **$29,047** (single, no kids) or adjusted family net
  income over **$36,309** (single parent, or married/common-law).
- No separate application; CRA checks when you file. Must be 19+ (or have a spouse/child) and an
  Ontario resident on Dec 31 (confirm age rule).
- **This one is exact enough to estimate.** Example: single student, net income $15,000 -> full $378.

### 2. Ontario Child Benefit (OCB)

- **Official, 2026-27:** up to **$146.66/month per child under 18** (about $1,760/year).
- Partial benefit when AFNI is above **$26,865** (reduction rate: 8% for 1 child... confirm on CRA).
- Paid **with the CCB** in one monthly payment. Applying for the CCB (RC66 for newcomers) is the
  application. Fully provincial money, CRA delivers it.

### 3. Ontario Low-income Workers Tax Credit (LIWTC, renamed from LIFT)

- **Official (ontario.ca):** lower of **$850** or **5.05% of employment income**.
- Reduced by 10% of adjusted individual net income over **$30,000**, or adjusted family net income
  over **$60,000**, whichever reduction is greater. Zero at about $38,500 single / $68,500 family.
- **Non-refundable:** it only cuts Ontario tax you owe. A student earning $12,000 who owes no Ontario
  tax gets nothing extra in cash. The app must say "reduces Ontario tax you owe" not "money you get".
- Needs employment income (self-employment doesn't count). Claimed on ON428-A.
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/low-income-workers-tax-credit

### 4. Ontario Childcare Access and Relief from Expenses (CARE) credit

- Refundable, top-up to the child care expense deduction for families with income up to about
  **$150,000**; worth up to 75% of eligible child care costs at low incomes (confirm current numbers).
  Claimed on the tax return. Relevant to student parents.
- $10-a-day child care (CWELCC): fees capped at **$22/day** since Jan 1, 2025, average about $19/day,
  target $10/day not yet reached (official ontario.ca/morechildcare). Not a "benefit to apply for" but
  a big money point for student parents: ask the daycare if it is in the program.

## B. Student aid

### 5. OSAP (Ontario Student Assistance Program)

- **Who (official):** Ontario residents who are **Canadian citizens, permanent residents or protected
  persons**. **International students cannot get OSAP.** Full-time and part-time. Need-based.
  Approved schools anywhere in the world.
- **Ontario resident (confirm definition):** generally lived in Ontario for 12 months in a row before
  starting school, not counting time as a full-time student. Defined on ontario.ca/page/osap-definitions.
- **Not eligible:** defaulted loans, overpayments, some bankruptcies, reached lifetime limit (340 weeks;
  400 doctoral; up to 520 with a disability).
- **Covers:** tuition, books and supplies, fees, living costs (full-time only), child care.
- **Big change, 2026-27 (official + secondary):** announced **February 12, 2026**, applies to programs
  starting on or after **August 1, 2026**:
  - The **provincial** part is now mostly **loans**. Secondary sources say the grant share dropped from
    up to **85% to 25%**; ontario.ca only says "more as loans". Show it as "most Ontario OSAP is now a loan".
  - **Career college** students: all provincial funding is loans (zero provincial grant).
  - The **federal** Canada Student Grant (up to $4,200) is **not** affected (see federal file), except
    that for-profit private school students mostly lost the federal full-time grant from Aug 1, 2026.
  - Tuition freeze lifted: domestic tuition can rise up to **2% a year for three years** (secondary).
  - Students already in a program before Aug 1, 2026 may be grandfathered (confirm).
- **Federal loan part is interest-free**; provincial part charges interest (confirm rate).
- **First-time applicants** must complete a ~15-minute interactive module first.
- **OSAP Aid Estimator:** https://osap.gov.on.ca/AidEstimator2324Web/enterapp/enter.xhtml (link from
  the app instead of trying to re-calculate OSAP ourselves; it's too complex and changes yearly).
- Links: https://www.ontario.ca/page/osap-ontario-student-assistance-program ,
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/learn-about-osap , https://www.ontario.ca/page/osap-definitions

### 6. Ontario Learn and Stay Grant

- Covers **full tuition, books and direct costs** for students in eligible **nursing, paramedic and
  medical lab** programs in under-served regions (north, east, southwest), in return for working in that
  region after graduating (secondary: about 6 months per funded year, otherwise it becomes a loan).
- Must be a citizen, PR or protected person and an Ontario resident. Apply through OSAP.
- New for 2026-27: medical lab programs at Canadore and Confederation; family-medicine stream for med
  students (details pending, announced Sept 2026).
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-learn-and-stay-grant

### 7. Other student money to point to

- **Bursary for Students with Disabilities (BSWD)** and Canada Student Grant for Services and Equipment,
  through OSAP (confirm current amounts).
- **School bursaries and scholarships**: every Ontario college and university has an emergency bursary
  and a financial aid office. The app should always say "Ask your financial aid office".
- **Indigenous students:** Post-Secondary Student Support Program (through the First Nation), Indspire.

## C. Work, training and newcomer programs

### 8. Ontario Bridging Participant Assistance Program (OBPAP)

- One-time bursary **up to $5,000** for tuition, books and equipment in an approved **bridge training
  program** for internationally trained professionals (official).
- Who (official, current): permanent residents, naturalized citizens, some refugee claimants, and some
  work permit holders whom IRCC has confirmed meet PR requirements.
- Apply through the financial aid office of the college/university running the bridge program, by
  **March 31** each year while enrolled.
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-bridging-participant-assistance-program

### 9. Better Jobs Ontario (formerly Second Career)

- Up to **$28,000** for training of one year or less, **$35,000** for programs over one year up to two
  years. Covers tuition, books, transport and a living allowance up to **$500/week** (official).
- Who: laid off and not working (or in a temporary survival job), or unemployed 12+ weeks (sources
  conflict: 12 weeks vs 6 months). People on EI, Ontario Works or ODSP can apply.
- Apply through an **Employment Ontario** service provider.
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/better-jobs-ontario

### 10. Employment Ontario (free)

- Free job search help, resume help, apprenticeship, Canada-Ontario Job Grant. Open to most people
  legally allowed to work in Ontario. Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/employment-ontario

## D. Health

### 11. OHIP (Ontario Health Insurance Plan)

- **Official:** **no waiting period** ("There is no longer a waiting period for OHIP coverage").
- Must be in Ontario 153 days in any 12 months, 153 of the first 183 days after moving, and make Ontario
  your primary home, **and** be one of: citizen, registered Indian Act status, **PR**, **PR applicant
  confirmed eligible by IRCC**, **work permit holder working full-time for an Ontario employer 6+ months**
  (spouse and dependants may qualify too), caregiver or seasonal agricultural worker program, convention
  refugee or protected person, some TRP holders, clergy.
- **International students are NOT listed** -> they use **UHIP** (university) or a college plan.
  A PGWP holder working full-time for an Ontario employer can become eligible (secondary).
- Apply **in person at ServiceOntario** with 3 original documents (status, residency, identity).
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/apply-ohip-and-get-health-card

### 12. OHIP+ (free prescriptions, age 24 and under)

- **Official:** 24 or younger, has OHIP, **not covered by a private plan**. No sign-up; show health card at
  the pharmacy. Covers 5,900+ drugs. Stops at 25th birthday.
- **Student union health plans are private plans**, so most full-time university/college students bill
  their student plan first (they can opt out of the student plan in some schools; confirm the effect).
- **Trillium Drug Program** for high drug costs relative to income (any age).
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/learn-about-ohip-plus

### 13. Healthy Smiles Ontario (kids' dental)

- Free dental for children **17 and under** in low-income families. **Official limits from July 1, 2026:**
  family net income **$29,065 (1 child), $31,265 (2), $33,465 (3)**, +$2,200 per extra child up to $44,465.
- Automatic for kids on Ontario Works/ODSP. Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/get-dental-care
- Adults: use the federal CDCP.

## E. Income support and housing (safety net)

### 14. Ontario Works (OW)

- Up to **$733/month** single (basic needs + shelter) (official). Rates not indexed since 2018 (secondary).
- Full-time students in OSAP-eligible programs generally aren't eligible (confirm). Refugee claimants
  can be. Apply online or via the municipality.
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-works

### 15. Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP)

- Up to **$1,436/month** single from July 1, 2026 (+1.9%) (official): $825 basic + up to $611 shelter.
- Ontario says the Canada Disability Benefit is not clawed back from ODSP (confirm).
- Link: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-disability-support-program

### 16. Ontario Electricity Support Program (OESP)

- Monthly credit on the hydro bill, about **$35 to $75/month** (higher $52 to $113 for electric heat,
  Indigenous households, medical devices), based on household size and after-tax income. Lasts 2 years,
  then reapply. Apply at https://ontarioelectricitysupport.ca (or through the OW/ODSP caseworker).
- Students renting where **hydro is in their own name** can apply; if hydro is included in rent, they can't.
- Ontario Electricity Rebate is automatic on every residential bill (no action needed).
- **LEAP** (Low-income Energy Assistance Program): one-time emergency help with overdue bills, through
  the local utility (confirm amounts).

### 17. Canada-Ontario Housing Benefit (COHB)

- Monthly rent help. **You can't apply directly in most regions**; you get offered it from the
  municipal social housing **wait list** (Toronto, Peel, Durham...). Must have filed taxes.
- The app should say: "Get on your city's social housing wait list" with the city's link.
- Link (Toronto): https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/housing-shelter/access-community-housing/rent-geared-to-income-subsidy/canada-ontario-housing-benefit/

### 18. Local / city programs (phase 2, by city)

- Toronto **Fair Pass** transit discount (TTC) for low-income adults (confirm 2026 discount).
- City recreation fee subsidies (Toronto Welcome Policy), library cards, food banks (student union food banks).
- **211 Ontario** (dial 2-1-1 or 211ontario.ca) lists all local services. Always link it.
