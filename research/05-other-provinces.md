# Other provinces (Phase 6)

Researched 2026-10-18. Benefit year July 2026 to June 2027 unless noted. Numbers are in
`data/<province>.yaml`; this file records where they came from and what's still uncertain.

## What each province adds

| Province | Money (with formula) | Health card for newcomers | Student aid | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BC | BC Family Benefit (exact), BC renter's tax credit $400 (exact) | MSP: rest of arrival month + 2 months wait | StudentAid BC | Income assistance. Climate action tax credit **ended April 2025**. |
| Alberta | Alberta Child and Family Benefit (base exact; working part's 15% rates unconfirmed) | AHCIP: register within 3 months | Alberta Student Aid | Income Support. CWB amounts differ in Alberta (no estimate shown). |
| Saskatchewan | Low-Income Tax Credit $460/$460/$181 (rate 3.029% derived from the official $39,345-$81,668 range) | Within about 3 months | Saskatchewan Student Aid | Income Support |
| Manitoba | Renters Affordability Tax Credit $625 (2026 tax year) | PRs can apply on arrival | Manitoba Student Aid | Rent Assist (income limit about $28,096 single, secondary), EIA. The CRA pays no Manitoba benefits. |
| Quebec | Solidarity tax credit (secondary numbers: $363 QST part, $172 alone, $746/$906 housing, $158/child, 6% over $43,195) | RAMQ: up to 3 months; refugees, protected persons, children exempt | AFE (Loans and Bursaries); no federal Canada Student Grant | Family allowance (Retraite Québec, up to $3,068/child), work premium, social assistance. Own tax return (TP-1). Temporary residents need 18 months for the solidarity credit. |
| New Brunswick | NB HST credit (exact), Child Tax Benefit $250/child under $20,000 | From first day if settled permanently | Student Financial Services | Social assistance |
| Nova Scotia | Affordable Living Tax Credit (exact), Child Benefit $1,525/child under $26,000 | PRs from outside Canada: first day | Student Assistance | Income Assistance |
| PEI | Essentials Benefit (replaced the sales tax credit; $310/$365, minimum $175), Child Benefit $410/$290 | Must live there 6 months and a day | Student Financial Services | Social assistance |
| Newfoundland and Labrador | Child Benefit $1,888-$2,310/child under $20,397, Income Supplement up to $520 | Coverage from arrival or approval | Student Financial Assistance | Income Support, NL Disability Benefit (not yet in the app) |

International students and health cards: covered in BC (6+ month permit, monthly fee), Alberta (12+
months), Saskatchewan, New Brunswick (if rules met), Nova Scotia (from the 13th month), PEI, and NL
(12+ months). Not covered in Ontario or Manitoba. Quebec: only students from countries with a social
security agreement.

## Territories (added 2026-10-20)

| Territory | Money | Health card for newcomers | Student aid | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yukon | Child Benefit $80.50/month per child, full up to $35,000 (the CRA gives no reduction rate, so "part of it" above) | Register in person on arrival; 3-month wait; study permits not covered; work permits of 1+ year (open or Yukon-specific) | The Yukon SFA office handles Canada Student Loans and Grants; Yukon Grant $195/week (2026-27), 170 weeks, only for students who went to high school in the Yukon or grew up there | Social Assistance (post-secondary students usually can't get it) |
| Northwest Territories | Child Benefit from the GNWT table ($815/$652 for one child under 6 / 6-17, totals for 2-4 children, then $367/$293 each), full under $30,000, straight line to $0 at $80,000 | From another province: first day of the third month; from abroad: the health office sets the date; permits of 1+ year with an NWT address | Own program (no Canada Student Loans or Grants): 12 continuous months of NWT residency; basic grant only if schooled in the NWT; remissible and repayable loans (up to $1,400/month) | Income Assistance (19+) |
| Nunavut | Child Benefit $58/month per child, full up to $22,065 | Usually the first day of the third month; permits of 1+ year with a Nunavut address | FANS (no Canada Student Loans or Grants): 12 months of Nunavut residency, citizens and PRs; grant for Nunavut Inuit, remissible loan for others; tuition up to $10,000, books $2,500 | Income Assistance (18+). Canada Workers Benefit amounts differ (like Alberta and Quebec): no estimate shown |

Federal, for everyone in the three territories: the northern residents deduction (line 25500), $11 a
day plus $11 more if nobody else in the home claims it, after 6 months in a row in the North.

Reading the NWT table for families with children of both ages: each column is looked up by its own
count of children (one under 6 and one aged 6-17 = $815 + $652). The handout doesn't show a mixed
family; check this if the GNWT publishes an example.

No other territorial credit is paid by the CRA in 2026: the CRA pages list only the child benefits
(the old carbon-tax rebates are gone).

Sites: yukon.ca and gov.nu.ca refuse scripts (a browser check page), so `check_links.py` marks them
"??"; open them in a browser. canada.ca and the NWT sites answer normally.
